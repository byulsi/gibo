# Research capability composition

Guru Research coordinates investment research; it does not replace every
domain procedure. Infer the evidence, data, method, and output the question
requires, then inspect the research skills, tools, and data connectors available
in the current host. Combine the smallest useful set of specialized
capabilities that materially improves the work.

Honor a user-specified capability when it fits the request. Availability alone
is not a reason to invoke a capability, and one specialized capability does not
imply a fixed workflow for its whole asset class. When a needed capability is
missing or inaccessible, state the resulting limitation and use the best
available alternative. Do not install, connect, or configure a missing
capability unless the user explicitly requests it and the host permits it.

Divide responsibilities clearly:

- the specialized capability owns its domain procedure, such as filing
  retrieval, market-data access, transformation, calculation, or backtesting;
- Guru Research owns question framing, project-memory retrieval, evidence
  evaluation, normalization, synthesis, uncertainty, and mode-gated retention;
  and
- the host agent owns tool selection, permissions, delegation, and final
  judgment.

Reuse adequate project evidence or already collected output instead of
repeating the same retrieval. Add an independent source or method only when it
provides a material cross-check, covers a gap, or tests a consequential
assumption.

Before combining outputs, normalize material differences in:

- as-of time and data cutoff;
- identifiers, venue, currency, units, and price construction;
- frequency, sampling window, benchmark, and return convention; and
- assumptions, parameters, transaction costs, and methodological scope.

Treat every capability result as evidence, not authority. Inspect its sources,
method, cutoff, assumptions, and limitations in proportion to the decision
stakes. For derived results, retain enough inputs, parameters, version or data
vintage, and validation detail to make the result reproducible.

Examples are illustrative, not routing rules:

- issuer or company research may pair official filings with a filing-oriented
  retrieval capability;
- technical or quantitative research may pair suitable market data with a
  calculation or backtesting capability; and
- macro, rates, FX, commodity, or crypto research may use authoritative current
  data plus a venue- or instrument-aware capability when one is available.
